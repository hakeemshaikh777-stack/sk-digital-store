Frequently asked questions

Everything important before you place an order.

Are these physical products?

No. SK Digital Store is designed for digital products. There is no physical shipping.

How do I receive my purchase?

After Razorpay confirms and the server verifies your payment, a secure download link is generated for the purchased digital product.

Can I buy from my phone?

Yes. The storefront is responsive and designed for touch screens as well as desktop browsers.

Is the payment system live?

The website contains the Razorpay checkout integration, but it will accept live payments only after you deploy the Node.js backend with your approved Razorpay live credentials.

What if I need support?

Use the Contact Us page for product or order-related questions.

Before you purchase

Review the product details and store policies. Digital delivery begins only through the configured post-payment flow.
