Refund & Cancellation Policy

Cancellation

A payment can generally be cancelled before it is completed. Once a digital order has been successfully paid and access has been issued, cancellation may not be possible.

Refund requests

Because products are digital, refund eligibility is assessed based on the circumstances. If a product is inaccessible, defective or materially different from its description, contact support promptly with the order details and a description of the issue.

Approved refunds

Where a refund is approved, it is returned through the relevant payment method, subject to payment-provider processing timelines.
